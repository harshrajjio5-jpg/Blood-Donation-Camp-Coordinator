About Our Project

Blood Donation Eligibility

✔ Age should be between 18 and 65 years.

✔ Minimum body weight should be 45 kg.

✔ Haemoglobin should be at least 12.5 g/dL.

✔ Donor should be in good general health.

Final eligibility depends on medical screening by qualified medical staff.
