About Us

Blood Donation Camp Coordinator is a platform designed to provide information about blood donation camps. Our aim is to encourage people to donate blood and spread awareness about the importance of blood donation. We help interested donors learn about upcoming camps, understand eligibility criteria and register for donation camps.

Blood Donation Eligibility

Before donating blood, check these general requirements:

  • Donor must be between 18 and 65 years old.
  • First-time donors should not be above 60 years.
  • Weight should meet the required medical criteria.
  • Hemoglobin level should be at least 12.5 g/dL.
  • Donor should be physically fit and healthy.

Final eligibility is decided by qualified medical staff after health screening.
