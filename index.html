Donor Eligibility Criteria

  • Generally, donors should be between 18 and 65 years old.
  • Donors must meet the required weight and haemoglobin criteria.
  • Donors should be in suitable health on the donation day.
  • Final eligibility will be checked by qualified medical staff.

Eligibility depends on medical screening and applicable guidelines.
